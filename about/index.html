About

John Ndigirigi

I'm John, a full-stack and AI engineer in Nairobi. For four years I've built production software for remote teams in the UK, Germany and elsewhere: SaaS platforms, mobile apps, and AI features that handle real money and real customer data.

Right now I'm building QuoteKamili, an AI quoting product for trade suppliers in the UK and US, and taking freelance work through Toptal. Before that I led engineering at Sociale, a multi-tenant finance platform for savings groups across Africa.

I started in identity and blockchain, building verifiable-credential and on-chain systems at DirectEd, Blocktrust and DIGN. That work taught me to design for correctness and security first, which is how I approach AI today.

I also teach. Most recently I tutored on the Cardano track at CATS 2026 (500+ developers across 12 African cities) and mentored teams at the Kenya AI Challenge.

Open to relocation for the right full-time role.
